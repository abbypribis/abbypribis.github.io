I am a first-year PhD student at MPI-SP, advised by [Cătălin Hrițcu](https://catalin-hritcu.github.io/).

My main interests lie in formal verification and type systems. 
Currently, I am exploring combining correctness and incorrectness reasoning into effect-modular verification frameworks for monadic programs.

Prior to joining MPI-SP, I pursued an MPhil in Advanced Computer Science at the University of Cambridge. I received my undergraduate 
degree from the University of New Mexico.
